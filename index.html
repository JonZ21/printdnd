A robot that clears the bed

Your printer finishes
at 2 a.m. It should
start again at 2:01.

When a job ends, the robot lifts the bed, takes the part out and sets the machine up for the next one. The gap between prints stops being dead time, and nobody has to be in the room.

Recorded · the robot's own gripper cameras · unedited apart from compression

01 The reset

Four moves,
then it goes again.

The robot works the printer the way a person would, because the printer was built for a person. Scroll to run the recording.

  1. 01

    Remove

  3. 03

02 The machine

03 Why it matters

The bed is the first thing.
It isn't the last.

Print farms solve throughput by buying more printers. Nobody has taken out the minute of human attention each machine needs between jobs. Take that out and how hard a printer runs stops depending on who's awake.

Running today

  • A BracketBot working alongside a Bambu Lab X1 Carbon
  • Teleoperated bed lifts and part pickups, recorded and logged
  • The episode being replayed on this page

Where it goes

  • The reset running without a person driving it
  • Swapping filament when a spool runs out
  • Queueing work across several machines

The right-hand column is what we're building toward, not what we're selling. Items move left when they actually work.
